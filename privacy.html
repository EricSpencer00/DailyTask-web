Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2025

Introduction

Daily Task ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Local app data: Tasks, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device (in the app sandbox) by default.
  • Optional sharing: If you enable Cloud sync (iCloud/CloudKit) or App Group sharing for widgets, the data you choose will be stored in your private iCloud account or the shared App Group container. You must opt in to these features.
  • Health data: We may write simple completion records to Apple HealthKit when you explicitly enable HealthKit integration. Health data is never uploaded to our servers or to iCloud by the app.
  • Permissions: We request permissions only when necessary — for notifications (to send reminders) and for HealthKit (to write completions). We do not request unnecessary permissions.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect personally-identifying information such as your name, email, phone number, or contact list.
  • We do not collect or transmit raw HealthKit samples or health trends to our servers.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data to third parties for advertising or profiling.

Cloud sync and App Groups

Cloud sync (iCloud via NSPersistentCloudKitContainer) and App Group sharing are optional. If you enable sync, your task list and preferences may be stored in your private iCloud account. You will be prompted and must explicitly opt in via the app's settings. Health data is never synced to iCloud.

Third-party services

We may use third-party libraries for crash reporting or analytics. Where used, we limit data collection to non-personal, aggregated usage information. We will disclose third-party services clearly in the app and in this policy when applicable.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect data stored locally and in transit. Because no system is fully secure, we cannot guarantee absolute protection, but we follow standard best practices.

Your choices

  • Disable HealthKit integration in Settings to prevent writes to Apple Health.
  • Disable Cloud sync or App Group usage to keep data only on your device.
  • Manage notification permissions in your device settings.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
